Recipe Card — photo layout options

The larger feed card (ExploreRecipeCard.tsx). This one does get image controls: a Change photo / Remove pill over the photo when one exists, and an Add photo dropzone when it's empty (shown for the owner only). Left column = with photo · right column = empty state. The green Add button (adds the recipe to your collection) stays where it is today.

A

Hero photo on top

A wide hero photo caps the card; creator avatar floats on it, content and the Add button sit below. Familiar and photo-forward. Image controls tuck into the bottom-right of the hero so they never compete with the title.
