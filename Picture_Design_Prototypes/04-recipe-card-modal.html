Recipe Card — photo design

The pop-up recipe card (RecipeDetailModal.tsx). As we cleared up, this is the full recipe view — so it uses the same side-by-side header as Detail Option B for consistency: photo beside the title, macros and meta, then the existing two-column ingredients & instructions and the modal's Close / Edit / View / Delete row. Owner gets Change / Remove controls on the photo; visitors just see it.

✓

Selected design — Detail B header, in the modal

Shown inside the dark modal backdrop it lives in. The header photo is restrained (portrait, ~300px) so recipe info stays front-and-center — matching the detail page one-to-one.

Dinner

🍛 Red Lentil Coconut Curry

4 servings👩‍🍳 10m⏱️ 35m
390 cal| 18g protein| 48g carbs| 14g fat

🥗 Ingredients

1 cup red lentils
1 can coconut milk
2 tbsp curry paste
1 onion, diced
2 cups spinach

📝 Instructions

1Sauté onion and curry paste until fragrant.
2Add lentils, coconut milk and 2 cups water.
3Simmer 20 min until lentils are soft.
4Stir in spinach; season and serve.
∅

Empty state (owner, no photo yet)
