Miso-Glazed Salmon Bowl

Dinner2 servings👩‍🍳 15m⏱️ 30m
520 cal| 34g protein| 41g carbs| 18g fat

🥗 Ingredients

2 fillets salmon
2 tbsp white miso
1 cup jasmine rice
1 avocado, sliced
2 cups baby spinach

📝 Instructions

1Whisk miso with mirin and brush over salmon.
2Broil 8–10 min until caramelized and flaky.
3Cook rice; fluff and divide into bowls.
4Top with salmon, avocado, spinach, sesame.

🍛 Red Lentil Coconut Curry

4 servings👩‍🍳 10m⏱️ 35m
390 cal| 18g protein| 48g carbs| 14g fat

🥗 Ingredients

1 cup red lentils
1 can coconut milk
2 tbsp curry paste
1 onion, diced

📝 Instructions

1Sauté onion and curry paste until fragrant.
2Add lentils, coconut milk and 2 cups water.
3Simmer 20 min until lentils are soft.
