Recipe Tile — photo layout options

The compact tile in the dashboard grid (RecipeCard.tsx). No image add/remove controls here — the photo just displays. Keeps the current category chip, name, per-serving macros, and the favorite / delete actions. Category color becomes a subtle accent since the photo now carries the visual weight.

A

Photo on top

The classic food-blog card: a 4:3 photo caps the tile, text sits calmly below on white. Reads instantly, scales well in a dense grid, and is the safest, most familiar choice.

Side thumbnail

A square thumbnail on the left, text on the right — a tidy, list-like row. The most compact option: fits more recipes per screen and keeps titles + macros on one readable line. Best if the dashboard leans toward scanning many recipes quickly.
